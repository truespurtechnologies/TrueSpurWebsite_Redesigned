## SECTION 6 — PROVEN TRACK RECORD

### Purpose
Build trust in TrueSpur as a company.

Balance company proof (60%) with founder proof (40%).

Provide third-party validation and measurable outcomes.

---

### Final Copy

**SECTION HEADLINE (H2):**  
Proven Track Record

---

### Company Proof (60% — Primary)

**SUBHEADLINE:**  
Active Product Studio

**BODY COPY:**  
We're not just talking about building products—we're actively building four right now. From healthcare platforms to AI tools, each product proves our model works and teaches us something we bring to yours.

**VISUAL ELEMENT:**  
4 product logos or mini cards (Clinax, Halo, TrueBill, TafsirAI)

**SUPPORTING MESSAGE:**  
Product studio model in action. Real products. Real users. Real learning.

---

### Founder Proof (40% — Secondary)

**SUBHEADLINE:**  
Government-Recognized Innovation

**GOVERNMENT AWARD (2-3 sentences):**  
Our founder won a competitive healthcare contract from the Government of India—beating multinational corporations with a solution built for real-world impact. This wasn't just recognition; it was validation that our approach to product development delivers measurable results.

**DIRECT TURNAROUND (1 sentence with metric):**  
When a critical healthcare platform was failing at 5% success rate, we stepped in and turned it around to 80%+ within months.

**SUPPORTING MESSAGE:**  
Third-party validation. Measurable outcomes. Proven expertise.

---

### Design Notes

**Section Structure:**
- Section headline: Poppins Extrabold (800), centered
- Two-column layout (or stacked on mobile)
- Company proof: Left column or top (60% visual space)
- Founder proof: Right column or bottom (40% visual space)

**Company Proof Design:**
- Visually prominent (larger, first position)
- 4 product logos in grid or horizontal layout
- Clean, modern presentation
- Emphasis on "active building"

**Founder Proof Design:**
- Supporting position (smaller, secondary)
- Government award: Certificate image or badge
- DIRECT metric: Highlighted (5% → 80%+)
- Clean, credible presentation

**Visual Balance:**
- 60% space/attention to company proof
- 40% space/attention to founder proof
- Clear visual separation between proof types
- Cohesive overall section design

**Content Limits:**
- Company proof: 4 products + brief explanation
- Founder proof: 2 achievements only (government + DIRECT)
- No long founder biography
- No excessive credentials
- No founder philosophy
