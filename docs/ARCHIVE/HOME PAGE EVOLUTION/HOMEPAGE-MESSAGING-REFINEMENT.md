## RISKS TO WATCH DURING IMPLEMENTATION

### Risk 1: Hero Headline May Feel Too Casual

**Risk Level:** LOW

**Mitigation:**
- Subheadline provides professional positioning clarity
- Body copy adds PM credentials and healthcare expertise
- If testing shows concern, fall back to Option 3: "We Build Products Every Day — Our Own and Yours"

**Monitor:** Bounce rate, time on page, scroll depth

---

### Risk 2: "From the Inside" May Need Clarification

**Risk Level:** LOW

**Mitigation:**
- Body copy immediately explains: "We build our own products"
- 4 products listed as proof
- If testing shows confusion, fall back to Option 2: "We Build Products Too"

**Monitor:** Heatmaps on section, scroll depth, conversion rate

---

### Risk 3: Portfolio Signal May Be Too Subtle

**Risk Level:** MEDIUM

**Mitigation:**
- Placement is critical (must appear after 4 products are shown)
- If testing shows visitors miss it, consider Option 10: "As we build our portfolio, we bring those learnings to every founder partnership."
- Could also add visual emphasis (different text color, icon, or border)

**Monitor:** User testing feedback, exit surveys, conversion rate

---

### Risk 4: Healthcare Expertise May Be Under-Emphasized in Hero

**Risk Level:** LOW

**Mitigation:**
- Subheadline explicitly states "deep healthcare expertise"
- Healthcare section appears early (Section 5)
- If testing shows healthcare founders are missing it, consider Option 5 from hero headlines: "From Healthcare Platforms to Yours — Built by Product Builders"

**Monitor:** Healthcare founder conversion rate vs. general founder conversion rate

---

### Risk 5: Messaging May Feel Repetitive Across Sections

**Risk Level:** LOW

**Mitigation:**
- Each section has distinct purpose:
  - Hero = emotional hook + positioning
  - Product Studio = differentiation + model explanation
  - Products = proof + portfolio signal
