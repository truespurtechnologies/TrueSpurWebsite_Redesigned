## REFINEMENT 2: REDUCED "15+ YEARS" REPETITION

### What Changed

**Limited "15+ years" to single usage in hero supporting line only.**

### New Section Added

**EXPERIENCE & CREDIBILITY GUIDANCE** (New major section)
- "15+ Years" Usage Strategy
- Where "15+ Years" SHOULD Appear (1 location)
- Where "15+ Years" Should NOT Appear
- Alternative Credibility Language
- Credibility Hierarchy
- Experience Messaging Rules

---

### "15+ Years" Usage Rules

**APPROVED USAGE (1 location only):**

**Hero Supporting Line:**
"Backed by 15+ years of product experience across healthcare and complex software platforms."

**Why here:**
- Establishes credibility early
- Supporting line (tertiary element, not primary)
- Paired with broader scope ("complex software platforms")
- One-time mention (not repeated)

---

### Where "15+ Years" Should NOT Appear

❌ Hero headline  
❌ Hero subheadline  
❌ Product Studio section  
❌ Products section  
❌ Process section  
❌ Multiple locations throughout homepage

---

### Alternative Credibility Language

**Instead of "15+ years" use:**
- ✅ "Deep expertise"
- ✅ "Extensive experience"
- ✅ "Proven experience"
- ✅ "Production experience"
- ✅ "Government-recognized"
- ✅ "Domain expertise"

**Examples:**
- Healthcare section: "Deep experience building healthcare technology"
- About page: "Extensive product experience across healthcare and enterprise platforms"
- Proof section: "Government-recognized for healthcare innovation"

---

### Credibility Hierarchy

**PRIMARY CREDIBILITY (What visitors see first):**
1. 4 internal products (company proof)
2. Product studio model (company proof)
3. 5-step process (company proof)

**SECONDARY CREDIBILITY (Supporting details):**
4. 15+ years experience (founder proof) — mentioned once in hero
5. Government award (founder proof) — proof section
6. DIRECT turnaround (founder proof) — proof section

**TERTIARY CREDIBILITY (About page depth):**
7. Full career timeline
8. Detailed healthcare expertise
9. PM background and strategic capability

---

### Experience Messaging Rules

**DO:**
- Lead with company proof (products, process, model)
- Mention "15+ years" once (hero supporting line)
- Use alternative language elsewhere ("deep expertise")
- Frame experience as supporting credential, not primary identity

**DO NOT:**
- Repeat "15+ years" multiple times
- Lead with founder experience
- Make experience the primary credibility signal
- Dominate homepage with founder credentials
