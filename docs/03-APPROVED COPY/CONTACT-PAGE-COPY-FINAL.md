## SECTION 1 — HERO

### Purpose
Welcome visitors. Set tone immediately. Reduce sales pressure.

### Final Copy

**HEADLINE (H1):**  
Let's Talk About What You're Building.

**SUPPORTING COPY:**  
Whether you're validating an idea or ready to build, we'd love to have a conversation. No sales pitch. No pressure. Just an honest discussion about your product and whether we're the right partner to help you build it.

**PRIMARY CTA:**  
Start a Conversation

---

## SECTION 2 — WHY START THE CONVERSATION

### Purpose
Explain who reaches out and why. Remove barriers to contact.

### Final Copy

**SECTION HEADLINE (H2):**  
Why Founders Reach Out

**BODY COPY:**  
Founders reach out to us at different stages—some are validating an idea, others are ready to build, and some are already building but need help navigating complexity.

You might be a non-tech founder who understands your domain but needs help translating vision into execution. You might be building in healthcare and need expertise in compliance, EHR integration, or clinical workflows. Or you might just want to talk to someone who thinks like a product manager, not a vendor.

Wherever you are in your journey, if you're thinking about building a product, we're here to talk.

---

## SECTION 3 — WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

### Purpose
Reduce primary anxiety. Build confidence through transparency.

### Final Copy

**SECTION HEADLINE (H2):**  
What Happens Next

**BODY COPY:**  
Here's exactly what to expect after you reach out:

**You fill out the form.**  
Tell us about what you're building. Just a few sentences—enough for us to understand the problem you're solving and who it's for.

**We respond within 24 hours.**  
Aswar (our founder) or a senior team member will reach out to schedule a conversation. We'll send a calendar link or suggest a few times that work.

**We have a 30-45 minute conversation.**  
No sales pitch. We'll ask about your product, your goals, and what you've validated so far. We'll share how we'd approach it, what concerns us, and what excites us. We'll challenge assumptions and help you think through the hard questions.

**We determine fit together.**  
Not every idea should be built. Not every founder-partner fit works. We'll be honest about whether we think we're the right partner for you. If we're not, we'll say so. If we are, we'll talk about next steps.

## SECTION 4 — YOU DON'T NEED TO BE READY

### Purpose
Remove psychological barriers. Use permission framework.

### Final Copy

**SECTION HEADLINE (H2):**  
You Don't Need Everything Figured Out

**BODY COPY:**  
You don't need a detailed spec. You don't need wireframes. You don't need a budget estimate or a technology stack picked out.

You just need to be thinking about a problem worth solving.

**If you're still validating:**  
We can help you figure out whether there's real demand, who your users are, and what to build first.

**If you're not sure where to start:**  
We can help you break down the problem, prioritize what matters, and create a roadmap that balances vision with reality.

**If you're worried about cost:**  
We'll have an honest conversation about what's realistic for your stage and goals. We're optimizing for building products that work, not maximizing project scope.

**If you're building in healthcare:**  
We understand the complexity—HIPAA compliance, EHR integration, clinical workflows, regulatory requirements. We've navigated it all for over a decade.

The best time to reach out is when you're thinking seriously about building something. We'll help you figure out the rest.

How We Help Founders

## FORM FIELD RECOMMENDATIONS

**Note:** This copy document does NOT include form field labels. These are implementation details for the design specification phase.

**Recommended Form Structure:**

**Required Fields (3):**
1. Name (single field)
2. Email
3. What are you building? (textarea, 3-5 sentences)

**Optional Fields (4 max):**
4. Company/Project Name
5. Where are you in your journey? (dropdown)
6. Timeline (dropdown)
7. How did you hear about us? (dropdown)

**Form CTA Button:** "Start a Conversation"

**Form Philosophy:**
- Minimal fields (enables conversation, doesn't replace it)
- Clear labels and helpful placeholders
- No budget or phone number fields
- Optional fields clearly marked
- Single-column layout
- Mobile-optimized
