---

### Optimization Notes

**V1 Issues:**
- Supporting statement felt generic: "Integrated into a product development process built for ambitious founders"
- Process card copy was too long (3-4 sentences each)
- Felt like process documentation, not homepage copy
- Some sentences were mechanical: "From wireframes to high-fidelity prototypes, we design interfaces that are intuitive, accessible, and conversion-focused"

**V2.0 Improvements:**
- Supporting statement: Added "who need speed and clarity" (founder pain point)
- Reduced each process card from 3-4 sentences to 2 sentences
- Made sentences punchier and more direct
- Removed jargon ("high-fidelity prototypes")
- Tightened language throughout

**V2.1 Improvements:**
- Further tightened all process cards for maximum scanability
- Made founder outcomes more explicit in each step:
  - Discover: "Reduce risk, save time, build the right thing"
  - Design: "converts visitors into users and users into advocates"
  - Build: "scale from launch to thousands of users without rebuilding"
  - Launch: "ready for real users, not just technically complete"
  - Scale: Streamlined to single sentence
- Reduced word count while increasing outcome clarity

**Reasoning:** Founders don't need process documentation on the homepage. They need confidence that you understand what they need: speed, clarity, and results. V2.1 makes outcomes explicit in every step.

## SECTION 5 — DEEP HEALTHCARE EXPERTISE

### Optimized Copy

**SECTION HEADLINE (H2):**  
Deep Healthcare Expertise

**BODY COPY:**  
Most teams hit a wall with healthcare technology. The regulations, the integrations, the compliance requirements—they stop projects cold.

We've navigated it all for over a decade: EHR integration, HIPAA compliance, HL7/FHIR standards, telemedicine regulations. Won government contracts. Rescued failing platforms. Built products serving thousands of patients.

That expertise makes us better builders—whether your product is in healthcare or not.

---

### Optimization Notes

**V1 Issues:**
- Two long paragraphs felt heavy
- "We've built healthcare products for over a decade, navigating the technical and regulatory challenges that stop most teams" = wordy
- "From winning competitive government contracts to rescuing failing healthcare platforms" = list format, not punchy

**V2.0 Improvements:**
- Broke into two tighter paragraphs
- Shortened first paragraph (removed redundancy)
- Made second paragraph punchier: "We've won. Rescued. Built." (three short statements)
- Removed "From...to" structure (felt formal)
- Kept technical depth but improved flow

**V2.1 Improvements:**
- Restructured to lead with outcome/challenge first: "Most teams hit a wall with healthcare technology"
- Technical details (EHR, HIPAA, HL7/FHIR) now appear as supporting proof, not opening statement
- Three-paragraph structure: Challenge → Proof → Value
- More human and accessible before becoming technical

**Reasoning:** Healthcare section needs to feel credible but not dominate. V2.1 leads with why visitors should care (the challenge) before proving expertise (the technical details).
