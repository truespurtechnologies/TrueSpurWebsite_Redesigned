## PART 5 – CRITICAL REVIEW OF ORIGINAL TACTICAL RECOMMENDATIONS

### Replace Contact Form with Calendly

**Decision:** ⏸️ **EXECUTE LATER** (Phase 3, Day 60+)

**Reasoning:**
- **Problem:** Solves wrong problem. TrueSpur doesn't have a booking efficiency problem. TrueSpur has a **lead quality problem**.
- **Risk:** Calendly without qualification = calendar full of unqualified leads
- **Better Approach:** Fix positioning and proof first. Add qualification criteria. THEN add Calendly.
- **When:** After 10+ qualified discovery calls to understand what "qualified" means

---

### Add Pricing Transparency

**Decision:** ❌ **DO NOT EXECUTE**

**Reasoning:**
- **Problem:** Dangerous without positioning. Will either (a) scare away qualified leads or (b) attract price shoppers
- **Reality:** Premium positioning requires price discovery, not price transparency
- **Healthcare Context:** Healthcare projects vary wildly (HIPAA compliance, FDA, medical device). Fixed pricing is impossible.
- **Better Approach:** "Typical engagements range from $50K-$250K depending on scope" (vague range, not specific pricing)
- **When:** Never. This is a custom service, not a productized offering.

---

### Create Lead Magnets

**Decision:** ⏸️ **EXECUTE LATER** (Month 4-6, if at all)

**Reasoning:**
- **Problem:** Premature. No authority established. No audience to distribute to.
- **Reality:** Lead magnets work when you have traffic and authority. TrueSpur has neither.
- **Better Approach:** Case studies ARE the lead magnet. Detailed, public case studies build authority.
- **When:** After 3 case studies are live, blog is active, and there's organic traffic to capture

---

### Launch Blog

**Decision:** ⏸️ **EXECUTE LATER** (Month 4-6)

**Reasoning:**
- **Problem:** Premature. Blogging requires consistent output and audience. TrueSpur has neither.
- **Reality:** Blogs are for distribution and authority. Positioning comes first.
- **Better Approach:** Focus on case studies (proof) before thought leadership (opinion)
- **When:** After positioning is clear, case studies are live, and there's a content strategy

---

### Add Quizzes and Assessments

**Decision:** ❌ **DO NOT EXECUTE**

**Reasoning:**
- **Problem:** Gimmick. Doesn't build trust or credibility.
- **Reality:** Healthcare founders don't want quizzes. They want proof you've solved their problem.
- **Better Approach:** Case studies, founder story, testimonials
- **When:** Never. This is a B2C growth hack, not a B2B trust builder.

---

### Add Chat Widgets

**Decision:** ❌ **DO NOT EXECUTE**

**Reasoning:**
- **Problem:** Distraction. Adds complexity without addressing core problem.
- **Reality:** Healthcare founders don't impulse-buy product development. They research, evaluate, and schedule calls.
- **Better Approach:** Clear CTA to book discovery call
- **When:** Never. This is for e-commerce, not high-touch B2B services.

---

### Build Email Nurture Sequences

**Decision:** ⏸️ **EXECUTE LATER** (Month 6+)

**Reasoning:**
- **Problem:** Premature. No leads to nurture. Need positioning and traffic first.
- **Reality:** Nurture sequences work when you have volume. TrueSpur needs quality over volume.
- **Better Approach:** Manual follow-up for first 20 leads. Learn what works. THEN automate.
- **When:** After 50+ form submissions and clear patterns emerge
